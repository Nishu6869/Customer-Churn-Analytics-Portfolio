A transparent rule-based risk score

Scoring rules

  • Tenure ≤6 months: +2; 7–12 months: +1
  • 4+ support tickets: +2; 2–3 tickets: +1

This is an explainable prioritization heuristic, not a trained or validated machine-learning model. Validate weights and thresholds on a larger historical dataset before operational use.
Recommended next steps · 06 / 06

Test targeted retention actions

  1. Month-to-month customers: offer a transparent plan/value review, especially in the first year.
  2. Repeated support contacts: trigger proactive follow-up, resolve recurring issues and confirm satisfaction.
  3. Early-tenure customers: provide onboarding and product-adoption check-ins.
  4. Measure a pilot: track churn, retention, monthly charges associated with churn and repeat support tickets before and after outreach.

Expected direction, not a guaranteed result

Earlier intervention may help reduce avoidable churn and improve support resolution. No numeric uplift is claimed because the dataset contains no intervention history or controlled experiment.

Key limitations: n=15, synthetic/sample data, currency unspecified, associations are not causal, and scoring rules need validation.
